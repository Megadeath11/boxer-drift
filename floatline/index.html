FLOATLINE

Two players, one keyboard and mouse. One of you is the balloon, the other builds the defense.

Balloon (keyboard)

Spend stat points on Health, Speed, Regrow, Jump and Camo (camo shrinks tower range).
WASD moves you along the track. You can weave side to side, but you can't leave it.
Space jumps over tacks, spikes and frost pulses. Shift dashes to slip past darts and mortar shells.
Reach the gate and every layer you have left is taken off its HP. You get +1 stat point after every run.

Defender (mouse)

Place towers beside the track. Spike Piles go on the track itself.
Click a tower to upgrade or sell it.
You earn money over time and for every layer you pop.
Hold out until the clock runs out to win. The balloon wins if the gate's HP hits zero.
